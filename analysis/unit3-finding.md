The dashboard for airline bookings shows 200 airline booking reservations. The data shows that 40% of bookings were made between February 2023 and June 2023. Four flights originate from Korea. 11% passengers cancelled due to no show, while 83% passengers have not cancelled their flights.

Problems were found when handling the filtering of no show passengers within the dashboard when filtering cancellation details. With certain settings it would reflect an incorrect number of no-show passengers. Including the appropriate information reflected the correct results. After this investigation it is proven that the dashboard is reflecting the correct information and the quartly payout can resume processing.
